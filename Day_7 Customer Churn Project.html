FINAL BUSINESS INSIGHTS

The Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) analysis successfully identified customer groups based on their long-term business revenue potential.

The analysis revealed that premium-value customers contribute significantly higher revenue to the business, making them the most critical customer segment for retention strategies.

A detailed churn and LTV analysis showed that:

  • Monthly charges, tenure, and total charges are the strongest drivers of customer lifetime value.
  • Premium-value customers with month-to-month contracts and fiber optic services showed higher churn tendencies.
  • Losing high-LTV customers can create substantial long-term revenue loss for the business.
  • Combining churn prediction with LTV analysis creates a more intelligent and business-driven customer retention system.

The project successfully evolved from a basic churn prediction model into a "revenue-focused customer intelligence system" capable of identifying both churn risk and customer business value simultaneously.
